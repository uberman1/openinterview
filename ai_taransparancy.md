# OpenInterview.me

## AI Transparency & Trust

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]
**Last Updated:** [Insert Date]

OpenInterview.me uses artificial intelligence (“AI”) and automated systems to help users create, analyze, and share professional profiles more efficiently. This page explains **how AI is used**, **what it does and does not do**, and **how we protect user trust**.

Our goal is transparency, user control, and responsible use.

---

## 1. Why We Use AI

AI allows OpenInterview.me to:

* Reduce manual work for users
* Improve clarity, structure, and formatting of content
* Generate transcripts and summaries from video and audio
* Provide suggestions to improve resumes and profiles
* Power scheduling and workflow automation

AI is used to **assist users**, not replace human judgment or decision-making.

---

## 2. What AI Is Used For

AI and automated tools may be used to:

* Transcribe video and audio recordings
* Analyze resumes for structure, clarity, and relevance
* Generate suggested edits or rewritten content
* Create summaries, highlights, or formatting improvements
* Optimize profile presentation for readability
* Support internal quality, safety, and performance monitoring

AI outputs are always optional and may be edited, rejected, or ignored by the user.

---

## 3. What AI Is NOT Used For

AI is **not** used to:

* Make hiring decisions
* Rank or score candidates for employers
* Determine employment eligibility
* Guarantee interviews or job offers
* Act as a recruiter, employer, or career advisor

All employment decisions are made independently by third parties.

---

## 4. Human Control & Responsibility

Users remain fully in control of their content.

* AI outputs do **not** publish automatically
* Users are responsible for reviewing all AI-generated content
* Users decide what to save, edit, delete, or share publicly
* Public profile visibility is always user-controlled

AI suggestions are **assistance**, not instructions.

---

## 5. Accuracy, Limitations & Bias

AI systems are probabilistic and may:

* Produce inaccuracies
* Omit context
* Reflect bias present in training data
* Generate content that may not fit every situation

For this reason:

* AI outputs should not be treated as factual guarantees
* AI content should not be relied upon as legal, employment, or professional advice

We continuously monitor and improve AI behavior, but no system is perfect.

---

## 6. Data Usage & Model Training

OpenInterview.me **does not use user content to train general-purpose AI models**.

User data may be processed by third-party AI providers **only to provide the requested service**, such as transcription or content optimization.

We do not sell AI-processed data or use it for unrelated training purposes.

---

## 7. Privacy & Security

AI processing follows the same privacy and security standards as the rest of the platform.

* Data is processed only as needed to deliver features
* Access is restricted to authorized systems
* We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards
* Public content is only public when a user chooses to share it

For more details, see our **Privacy Policy**.

---

## 8. Fair Use & Ethical Standards

We design AI usage to align with:

* User benefit
* Transparency
* Minimization of harm
* Respect for user autonomy
* Compliance with applicable laws and regulations

We do not knowingly deploy AI in ways intended to deceive, manipulate, or discriminate.

---

## 9. Feedback & Accountability

We welcome feedback on AI behavior.

If you believe:

* AI output is incorrect or harmful
* Content violates platform rules
* A system is behaving unexpectedly

Please contact us so we can investigate and improve.
