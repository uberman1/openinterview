AI Transparency & Trust

Understanding how we use AI to help you succeed.

In Plain English

check_circle AI helps you work faster.
check_circle You stay in control.
check_circle We don’t sell your data.
check_circle We don’t make hiring decisions.

OpenInterview.me uses artificial intelligence (“AI”) and automated systems to help users create, analyze, and share professional profiles more efficiently. This page explains how AI is used, what it does and does not do, and how we protect user trust.

Our goal is transparency, user control, and responsible use.

1. Why We Use AI

AI allows OpenInterview.me to reduce manual work, improve clarity, generate transcripts, and provide suggestions. AI is used to assist users, not replace human judgment.

2. What AI Is Used For

  • Transcribe video and audio recordings
  • Analyze resumes for structure and clarity
  • Generate suggested edits
  • Create summaries

AI outputs are always optional and may be edited, rejected, or ignored by the user.

3. What AI Is NOT Used For

AI is not used to:

  • Make hiring decisions
  • Rank or score candidates for employers
  • Determine employment eligibility
  • Guarantee interviews

4. Human Control & Responsibility

Users remain fully in control. AI outputs do not publish automatically. You decide what to share.

5. Accuracy & Limitations

AI systems are probabilistic and may produce inaccuracies or omit context. AI outputs should not be treated as factual guarantees.

6. Data Usage & Model Training

OpenInterview.me does not use user content to train general-purpose AI models. Data is processed only to provide the requested service.

7. Privacy & Security

AI processing follows our strict privacy and security standards. Data is encrypted and access is restricted.

8. Contact

For AI-related questions: trust@openinterview.me
