SMS Opt-In Verification

This page provides public verification of OpenInterview.me's transactional SMS opt-in workflow for compliance review.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy SMS Consent

1. How SMS Consent Works

Users begin by uploading their resume to auto-fill their profile. After completing the upload flow, they are taken to the profile editing page where they may optionally enter their phone number and check an unchecked SMS consent checkbox. Consent is never assumed or pre-checked.

2. Consent Language

The following exact consent text is displayed adjacent to the SMS opt-in checkbox on the profile editing page:

☐

Receive SMS notifications

I agree to receive transactional SMS notifications from OpenInterview.me regarding interview requests, interview scheduling, booking confirmations, reminders, and scheduling changes. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent is not required to use OpenInterview.me.

3. Compliance Summary

  • ✓ Transactional messages only
  • ✓ No promotional or marketing SMS

4. Message Types

OpenInterview.me sends only transactional SMS messages. Message types include:

  • Interview requests
  • Scheduling confirmations
  • Booking confirmations
  • Appointment reminders
  • Scheduling changes or cancellations
  • Account transactional notifications

No promotional or marketing SMS messages are sent.

5. Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies based on your account activity — for example, a user with frequent interview bookings will receive more messages than one with occasional activity. Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.

6. Opt-Out / Unsubscribe

Reply STOP to any SMS from OpenInterview.me at any time. You will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent. You can re-enable SMS notifications at any time from your profile settings. Opting out does not affect your ability to use any feature of OpenInterview.me.

7. Help

Reply HELP to any SMS from OpenInterview.me for assistance. You may also contact us directly at support@openinterview.me.

8. Data Privacy

Mobile opt-in data, including phone numbers collected for SMS notifications, is never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes. It is used solely to send the transactional messages described above. For full details see our Privacy Policy.

For questions about SMS consent or this compliance page, contact us at support@openinterview.me or 8 The Green STE A, Dover, DE 19901.
