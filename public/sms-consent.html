SMS Consent

Transparency regarding how OpenInterview.me handles SMS opt-in consent.

OpenInterview users may optionally opt in to receive transactional SMS notifications from OpenInterview.me.

Consent Language

When opting in, users are presented with the following consent language:

Message Types

SMS messages sent by OpenInterview.me are transactional only and relate to:

  • Interview requests
  • Interview scheduling confirmations
  • Booking confirmations
  • Interview reminders
  • Scheduling changes
  • Account-related transactional notifications

We do not send promotional, marketing, or advertising SMS messages.

Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies based on your activity on the platform.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless carrier.

Opt-Out / Unsubscribe

You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any SMS message to unsubscribe immediately.
  • You will receive a confirmation that your number has been removed.
  • No further SMS messages will be sent after opt-out.

Help

Reply HELP to any SMS message to receive support information, or contact us at support@openinterview.me.

Data Privacy

Mobile opt-in information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS consent is not required to use OpenInterview.me.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
