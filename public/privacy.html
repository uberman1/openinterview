Information We Collect

When you use OpenInterview.me, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Name and contact information — your full name, email address, and optionally your phone number.
  • Profile content — professional bio, job title, location, and other information you choose to share on your public profile.
  • Video content — profile videos you record or upload to the platform, hosted via Cloudinary.
  • Resume — documents you upload to auto-fill your profile fields.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and interaction timestamps, collected to improve the service.
  • Device and browser information — IP address, browser type, and operating system for security and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and display your public interview profile.
  • To send transactional notifications about interview requests, scheduling, booking confirmations, and reminders.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To improve and maintain the platform, diagnose errors, and monitor security.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

SMS Notifications

SMS notifications are entirely optional. You may provide your phone number and check the SMS consent checkbox on your profile editing page to opt in. Opting in is not required to use OpenInterview.me. We send transactional messages only — no promotional or marketing SMS. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. Message & data rates may apply. See our SMS Consent page for full details.

Video Content

Profile videos are uploaded directly to Cloudinary, our third-party video hosting provider, using server-signed URLs. Videos are stored on Cloudinary's infrastructure and served via their CDN. Cloudinary may process video data in accordance with their own privacy policy. By uploading a video you grant OpenInterview.me a license to display it on your public profile. You may delete your video at any time from your profile settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service providers — Cloudinary (video hosting), Twilio (SMS), Stripe (payments), and infrastructure providers, under data processing agreements.
  • Law enforcement — when required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Mobile opt-in information and phone numbers are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 90 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or compliance purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@openinterview.me.
